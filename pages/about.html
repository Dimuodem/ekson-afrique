About Ekson Afrique

Ekson Afrique Ltd, founded in 2018, is a service provider company specializing in engineering, security systems, energy solar solutions, and general branding and supplies. With over a decade of experience, we deliver innovative, sustainable, and customized solutions.

Mission

To provide high-quality, reliable, and sustainable solutions that empower our clients to achieve their goals while promoting environmental stewardship and operational efficiency.

Vision

To be the preferred procurement partner in Kenya, driving innovation and sustainability in engineering, energy, and branding solutions while fostering long-term partnerships.
